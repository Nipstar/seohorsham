AI SEO and GEO

Getting quoted by AI is work you can scope, not buy

AI SEO and GEO are how a Horsham firm becomes easier for AI to understand and quote. AI SEO covers Google and its AI Overviews, and generative engine optimisation (GEO) covers ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini. Andy prices both to the size of the job. He cannot guarantee a citation, and nothing can be promised about what Google's AI answers will say, so the checks simply record what is there.

AI SEO

  • Keyword and content strategy built around the questions buyers ask AI

GEO (generative engine optimisation)

  • Key pages restructured into clear, quotable answers

What Horsham firms ask about AI and maps

Can you get me into Google's AI answers?

No one can promise that. Google decides what appears, drawing on things like reviews, a complete profile and clear facts on your site. The service works on those signals, and Andy will not claim more than that.

Would you take on a firm in Billingshurst or Southwater?

Yes. Both can sit on your report alongside Horsham and any other villages you serve.

What do I need to do each month?

Very little. You approve the posts and, if you want to, reply to the odd review yourself.

What is GEO, and is it different from local SEO?

GEO means generative engine optimisation: preparing your pages, facts and structured data so AI tools can read and quote them. Local SEO centres on the Google map and your profile. They are separate pieces of work, and GEO is priced to scope.

Will AI SEO change my map rankings?

Not directly, and it is not sold as if it will. AI SEO works on your website and how it is read by Google and AI Overviews, whereas the map depends mostly on your profile, reviews and location.

Do I need a new website for any of this?

Not necessarily. If yours is thin or out of date, Horsham website design comes from the same business, starting at £600.

Is there a contract?

You and Andy agree the terms in writing before any work begins.
